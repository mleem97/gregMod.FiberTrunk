## Long-distance trunk recipe (the motivating use case)

Two fiber panels across the building, one 24fo trunk, one walked route:

1. Set **Strands per trunk = 24** (F1 Mod Config or the F4 `24` preset).
2. Click a free port on fiber **panel A**, walk the tray/ladder route once to
   fiber **panel B**, click a free port there.
3. The mod creates the remaining 23 strands on neighbouring free ports of
   A and B.

One walk, one thick cable on screen, 24 strands in the game — like a
real-life 24fo trunk, but each strand stays individually usable.
